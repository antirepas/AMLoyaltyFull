### Loyalty Platform for Cafés (2024–2025)

Built a full-stack digital loyalty platform for cafés together with my co-founder, Marius. I was responsible for the complete technical implementation, including system architecture, backend, frontend, database design, authentication, and the loyalty functionality, while my co-founder focused on the business side.

The project was completed in October 2025 and resulted in a fully functional product (although not fully production-ready). Beyond software engineering, the project gave me hands-on experience with product design, entrepreneurship, and collaborating in a small startup environment.

The biggest lesson from this project was learning the importance of market validation. We made the classic mistake of building a complete product before confirming that customers actually wanted it. Although we successfully developed the application, we had no established sales process, customer acquisition strategy, or validated demand. Going through that failure fundamentally changed how I think about building products: validating assumptions and understanding customers now come before writing code.

Attempting to commercialize the product also introduced me to sales operations and business processes, including lead generation, cold outreach, CRM workflows, product demonstrations, onboarding, and customer success planning. These lessons directly inspired my next project, JupSend, where I applied a much more structured, validation-first approach.

The project also taught me the importance of clear ownership, planning, accountability, and structured project management when working with a partner. While we faced challenges coordinating work in an early-stage startup, the experience significantly improved my ability to collaborate, organize development, and think beyond software engineering.
